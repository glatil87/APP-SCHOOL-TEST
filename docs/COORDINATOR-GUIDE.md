# School Lost & Found — Coordinator guide

You are the app's coordinator. You decide who can join, and you can tidy up
if something goes wrong. Everything below is done from your phone.

App address: **app-school-test.vercel.app**

---

## Getting started (once)

1. Open the app address and tap **Start here** at the bottom of the sign-in
   page ("Setting the app up for the first time?").
2. Enter the school's name and your own details. You're now the coordinator.
   The setup page closes itself as soon as this is done.

## Inviting parents

1. On the home screen, tap **Members & invite links**.
2. Optionally type a label (e.g. "Year 3 WhatsApp") so you remember where you
   shared it, then tap **Create invite link**.
3. Tap **Copy link** or **Share…** and post it in a trusted parents' group.
   The link is only shown once — create a new one any time.
4. When you no longer need a link, tap **Switch off** next to it. People who
   already joined are not affected.

Anyone with a link can *ask* to join — nobody gets in until you approve them.

## Approving parents

- New requests appear under **Waiting for approval**, with the parent's name,
  their child's first name and their email. The home screen shows a
  "waiting" badge when someone is waiting.
- Tap **Approve** if you recognise them, or **Decline** if not.
- If you're unsure who someone is, check with them before approving.

## Looking after members

On the Members page, for each parent you can:

- **Reset password** — if someone forgets their password. You get a
  temporary password (like `kq7m-p2xd-h9wt`) to pass on privately. They can
  change it on their account page after signing in.
- **Make coordinator** — give another trusted parent the same powers as you.
  It's a good idea to have two coordinators, in case one is away.
- **Remove** — takes away someone's access straight away. You can restore it
  later from "Removed or declined".

## Removing a report

If a report is unsuitable (for example, a photo showing a child, or a
duplicate), open it and scroll to the bottom: **Remove this report**. This
deletes it and its photo for everyone and can't be undone.

## What parents can and can't see

- Only approved parents can see anything.
- Parents see each other's first name, child's first name and picture.
- Email and phone number are only shown to the two parents of a confirmed
  match (and to coordinators on the Members page).
- Matched and returned items disappear from the lists automatically.
- Reports that stay open for more than 2 months move to the "Older (2+
  months)" filter on the Missing and Found lists.

## Things to remind parents

- Photograph items only — **never children**.
- Mention name labels, stickers or marks: they make matches much easier.
- Check back now and then: new suggestions appear as others report items.
- There's a **How does this app work?** link at the bottom of the home screen.

## If something goes wrong

- Someone can't sign in → **Reset password** for them.
- Someone joined who shouldn't have → **Remove** them.
- A link was shared too widely → **Switch off** that link and create a new one.
- The app looks broken → take a screenshot and send it to whoever looks after
  the app for you.
